# Provenance

The style prompts and example boards come from the NotebookLM Slide Style Gallery curated by KUMIKO SHIRAKI (https://notebooklm-slide-gallery.shirakippt.chatgpt.site/). Per-style source links (note article, X post, original image) are kept in `styles.json`.

The Chinese translations were machine-translated with revised category, style-name and design-term wording by the collection's compiler. Example boards were re-encoded from PNG to JPEG for size; their content is unchanged.

The prompts and images remain the work of their original author. This library is bundled for local, personal use with the ppt-studio skill; credit the original author when sharing decks that closely follow a library style, and do not redistribute the library itself.
