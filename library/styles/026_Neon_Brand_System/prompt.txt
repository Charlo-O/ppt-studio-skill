Layout:
Style:
- Editorial publishing layout
- Modular grid composition
- Strong whitespace
- Clean rectangular image framing
- Asymmetrical balance
- Minimal luxury aesthetic
- Brand guideline document style

Grid:
Columns: 12
Margin: "Wide editorial spacing"
Alignment: "Strict geometric alignment"
Spacing: "Open and breathable"

Composition:
- Use oversized typography with elegant spacing
- Combine serif headlines and condensed sans-serif titles
- Arrange images inside modular rectangular grids
- Use small navigation text at top edges
- Keep large empty areas for visual calmness
- Align all elements precisely to grid lines
- Use accent color only for emphasis blocks or keywords

Treatment:
- Fashion editorial photography
- Soft natural lighting
- Muted tones
- Minimal compositions
- Luxury campaign visuals
- Occasional monochrome photography
- Tight crop portraits mixed with wide editorial shots

Graphic Elements:
- Thin divider lines
- Small navigation headers
- Editorial page markers
- Modular image frames
- Minimal data blocks
- Large typography compositions
- Accent-color rectangles and bars

Rules:
- Do not use gradients
- Do not use glossy UI effects
- Avoid corporate infographic styling
- Use strong whitespace throughout
- Keep compositions calm and structured
- Maintain editorial magazine aesthetics
- Use accent green sparingly for impact
- Prioritize typography hierarchy over decoration
- Avoid rounded cards and soft shadows
- Keep layouts geometric, clean, and luxurious
