Visual Rules:
- Use one dominant accent color per slide.
- Maintain generous whitespace.
- Combine bold color blocks with clean white space.
- Use circular motifs consistently throughout the presentation.
- Keep layouts playful while preserving strong readability.
- Alternate between photo-driven and infographic-driven slides.
- Favor flat graphics over realistic 3D effects.

Avoid:
- Glassmorphism
- Skeuomorphic textures
- Heavy shadows
- Metallic effects
- Dark corporate themes
- Dense text
- Excessive decorative illustrations
