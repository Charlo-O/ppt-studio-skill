Rules:
- Combine editorial sophistication with friendly accessibility.
- Use bold condensed typography to establish hierarchy.
- Maintain abundant whitespace between infographic modules.
- Apply pastel accent colors sparingly for emphasis.
- Use long shadows consistently across icons and illustrations.
- Keep all visual elements flat and lightweight.
- Avoid realistic rendering and heavy decorative effects.
- Ensure charts, icons, and diagrams share a unified visual language.
- Optimize for quick scanning and intuitive understanding.
- Suitable for workshops, education, startup presentations, internal communications, marketing explainers, and magazine-style reports.
