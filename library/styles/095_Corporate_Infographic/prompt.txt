Rules:
- Prioritize clarity over decoration.
- Use accent colors sparingly to emphasize key data.
- Maintain strict alignment throughout the slide.
- Ensure all charts share a unified visual language.
- Avoid 3D effects and skeuomorphic elements.
- Present information in modular blocks for easy scanning.
- Use large numeric figures as visual anchors.
- Keep illustrations simple and functional.
- Suitable for consulting reports, annual reports, dashboards, business reviews, and strategy presentations.
