Rules:
- Use monochromatic neumorphic surfaces as the foundation.
- Reserve gradient accents for moments of emphasis.
- Balance premium minimalism with visual warmth.
- Highlight people through circular portraits and gradient frames.
- Maintain generous whitespace throughout.
- Keep charts executive-focused and easy to scan.
- Use embossed typography sparingly for sophistication.
- Avoid visual clutter and excessive decoration.
- Ensure every element follows a unified depth system.
- Suitable for AI presentations, SaaS proposals, executive briefings, consulting decks, startup pitches, annual reports, and premium business storytelling.
