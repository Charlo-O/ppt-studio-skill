Rules:
- Use aurora glass forms as the signature visual motif.
- Limit decorative objects to preserve elegance.
- Emphasize large typography and generous whitespace.
- Present information with minimal visual noise.
- Use frosted glass panels for hero sections and KPI slides.
- Reserve purple accents for numbers, highlights, and progress indicators.
- Avoid heavy charts, dense layouts, and excessive decoration.
- Maintain a calm, scientific, and premium atmosphere.
- Ensure all slides share a cohesive glassmorphism aesthetic.
- Suitable for healthcare presentations, biotech reports, wellness topics, AI strategy decks, scientific storytelling, premium consulting presentations, and future-oriented keynote presentations.
