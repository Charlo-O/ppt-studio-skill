Visual Rules:
- Every slide should resemble a premium editorial magazine.
- Cats should be treated as magazine cover models.
- Mix clean magazine layouts with chaotic internet meme imagery.
- Use oversized headlines and minimalist typography.
- Incorporate reaction stickers naturally into the layout.
- Alternate between full-bleed image spreads and clean article pages.
- Preserve the humor of meme culture while maintaining premium editorial aesthetics.
- Create the feeling of reading a trendy Gen Z culture magazine.
