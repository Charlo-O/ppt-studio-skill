Design Style:
Name: "Neon Editorial Minimal"
Concept: "ネオンカラーとクラシックなセリフ体を組み合わせた、雑誌風の大胆でモードなプレゼンデザイン"

Canvas:
Ratio: "16:9"

Mood:
- Bold
- Editorial
- Fashion
- Neon
- Minimal
- Modern
- Energetic
- Creative

Color Palette:
Background:
Main: "#F7F7F2"
Light: "#FFFFFF"
Dark: "#0B0B0B"

Text:
Primary: "#0B0B0B"
Secondary: "#555555"
Light: "#FFFFFF"

Accent:
NeonLime: "#C8FF00"
ElectricPurple: "#9B7BFF"
VividOrange: "#FF7A00"
SoftPink: "#F7B6C8"

Typography:
Heading:
Font: "Classic Editorial Serif"
Weight: "Bold"
Style: "Large, dramatic, magazine-title style"
Usage: "Use oversized serif titles as the main visual element"

Body:
Font: "Clean Sans Serif"
Weight: "Regular"
Style: "Small, compact, structured"

Label:
Font: "Condensed Sans Serif"
Weight: "Bold"
Style: "Uppercase, tiny captions and section labels"

Layout:
Style:
- Editorial magazine spread
- Large typography
- Strong asymmetry
- Wide margins
- High contrast color blocks
- Minimal text density
- Photo-based accent composition

Grid:
Columns: 12
Margin: "Generous"
Alignment: "Strict but playful"
Spacing: "Wide negative space with compact information blocks"

Composition:
- Use one oversized headline per slide
- Place small metadata text in corners
- Use bright neon rectangles as visual anchors
- Combine black-and-white or fashion-style photos with neon frames
- Allow titles to overlap image areas slightly
- Use thin lines and small labels for editorial detail

Image Style:
Treatment:
- Fashion editorial photography
- Cropped portrait or lifestyle images
- High contrast
- Clean rectangular framing
- Neon color overlays or borders

Graphic Elements:
- Neon color blocks
- Thin black divider lines
- Small page numbers
- Tiny uppercase captions
- Minimal table-of-contents lists
- Large numeric highlight cards

Slide Types:
Title Slide:
Layout: "Huge serif title with neon background or dark minimal background"

Contents Slide:
Layout: "Clean white background, compact list, small image accent"

About Slide:
Layout: "Text-heavy editorial block with one neon-framed image"

Project Detail Slide:
Layout: "Large image on one side with neon title block"

Case Study Slide:
Layout: "Dark background, neon headline, photo collage"

Data Slide:
Layout: "Stacked neon statistic cards with large numbers"

Rules:
- Do not use decorative gradients
- Do not use rounded corporate cards
- Keep text blocks small and structured
- Use strong contrast between serif titles and tiny sans-serif labels
- Keep the overall look editorial, bold, and fashion-oriented
- Use neon colors as accents, not as full background on every slide
