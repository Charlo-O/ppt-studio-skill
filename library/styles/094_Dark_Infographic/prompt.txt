Rules:
- Use bold condensed typography as the primary visual anchor.
- Organize each slide into independent infographic modules.
- Maintain a consistent dark editorial aesthetic throughout.
- Use vibrant accent colors only to highlight important information.
- Ensure all icons, charts, and illustrations follow a unified flat vector style.
- Avoid realistic rendering, heavy shadows, and 3D effects.
- Balance dense information with generous spacing for readability.
- Prioritize quick scanning and intuitive understanding.
- Suitable for annual reports, strategy decks, business proposals, dashboards, and magazine-style presentations.
