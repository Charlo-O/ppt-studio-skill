Design Rules:
- Every slide should feel like a TV title sequence for internet culture.
- Cats are always the heroes of the composition.
- Treat reaction cats as celebrities or pop idols.
- Layer PNG stickers, captions, and graphic elements dynamically.
- Use bold asymmetrical layouts with energetic movement.
- Mix premium editorial typography with chaotic meme imagery.
- Alternate between full-bleed hero images and collage compositions.
- Make every slide resemble a viral social media campaign or MTV broadcast package.
