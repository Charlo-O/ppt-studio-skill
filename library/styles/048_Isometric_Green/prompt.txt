Design Style:
Name: "Isometric Business Infographic"

Concept: >
戦略資料、提案書、営業資料、
事業計画、マーケティング資料、
研修資料、社内報告資料など、
幅広いビジネススライド向けの
アイソメトリックインフォグラフィックデザイン。

データ、プロセス、フレームワーク、
組織、プロジェクト、サービスなどを
アイソメトリックイラストと図解で
わかりやすく整理して伝える。

情報の理解しやすさを最優先にしながら、
モダンで親しみやすい印象を与える。

Canvas:
Ratio: "16:9"

Mood:
- Professional
- Friendly
- Structured
- Informative
- Strategic
- Clean
- Modern
- Approachable

Background:

Layout:
Style: "Asymmetrical Infographic"

Rules:
- Large title aligned top-center or top-left
- One large isometric illustration occupies 30–50% of slide area
- Content blocks arranged around illustration
- Generous whitespace
- Strong visual hierarchy through numbering
- Avoid dense paragraphs
- Prefer 3–6 content items per slide

Illustration:
Type: "Flat Isometric Vector"

Subjects:
- Business teams
- Office workspaces
- Strategy maps
- Dashboards
- Analytics
- Process diagrams
- Customer journeys
- Digital platforms
- Laptops
- Presentation boards
- Growth charts
- Collaboration tools
- Project management
- Innovation concepts

Style:
- Geometric
- Simplified
- Soft shadows
- Clean vector shapes
- Minimal detail
- Consistent perspective

Components:
Section Cards:
Shape: "Rounded Rectangle"
Background: "#FFFFFF"
Border: "None"

Number Markers:
Shape: "Circle"
Style: "Solid Accent Color"
TextColor: "#FFFFFF"

Connectors:
Style: "Thin Lines"
Color: "#86B25C"

Icons:
Style: "Isometric"
Color: "Palette Matched"

Slide Types:
- Cover
- Executive Summary
- Agenda
- Overview
- Timeline
- Process Flow
- Roadmap
- Framework
- Four-Column Overview
- Section Divider
- Feature Explanation
- Comparison
- Organization
- KPI Dashboard
- Summary

Visual Rules:
- Use one dominant isometric object per slide
- Pair illustration with numbered content blocks
- Maintain consistent isometric angle across all slides
- Use accent colors only for emphasis
- Keep backgrounds plain and uncluttered
- Emphasize readability over decoration
- Favor infographic layouts over traditional bullet lists

Avoid:
- Photographs
- Realistic rendering
- Dark backgrounds
- Heavy gradients
- Complex textures
- Excessive icon variety
- Dense text walls
