Design Rules:
- Every slide should communicate through visual metaphor instead of explanation.
- Cats should become impossible objects, landscapes, or characters.
- Combine unrelated subjects fearlessly.
- Treat internet memes as museum-quality artworks.
- Use one dominant surreal image per slide.
- Leave generous empty space around the composition.
- Typography should support the artwork, never overpower it.
- Make each slide feel like an exhibition poster from a contemporary art museum.

Avoid:
- Corporate presentation layouts
- Literal illustrations
- Business infographics
- Dense text
- Realistic compositions
- Stock photography
- Technical diagrams
- Cute pet photo albums
