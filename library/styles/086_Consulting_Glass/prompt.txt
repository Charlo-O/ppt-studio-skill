Rules:
- Every slide should contain a dominant glass panel.
- Use only 3–5 accent colors throughout the deck.
- Keep charts simple and executive-focused.
- Favor frameworks over dense data tables.
- Use large condensed typography for titles.
- Maintain strong symmetry and visual balance.
- Background gradients must remain soft and blurred.
- Icons should be outline style only.
- Suitable for consulting proposals, business plans, strategy decks, market analysis, DX initiatives, AI transformation proposals, and executive presentations.
