Layout:
General:
- Use elegant editorial grid layouts
- Maintain large negative space
- Use soft asymmetrical balance
- Keep compositions minimal and calm
- Use refined alignment and spacing
- Prioritize breathing room over density

Structure:
- Use split layouts with text and photography
- Combine serif headlines with small sans-serif details
- Alternate light and dark section slides
- Use large image panels with subtle crop framing
- Keep information modular and highly curated

Composition:
- Place headings near upper-left corners
- Use thin divider lines and subtle captions
- Use wide horizontal layouts
- Add editorial spacing between content blocks
- Keep typography visually delicate

Visual Elements:
Imagery:
- Use monochrome and muted-tone fashion photography
- Use soft editorial portrait imagery
- Prefer natural lighting and calm compositions
- Use minimal image counts per slide
- Apply subtle grain or matte texture feeling

Shapes:
- Thin-line frames
- Minimal rectangular image masks
- Soft color panels
- Delicate separators
- Clean geometric alignment

Charts & Infographics:

Colors:
- Use muted blue and warm yellow as highlights

- Discovery and insights layouts
- Audience strategy pages
- Goals and purpose slides

- Research and estimate sections
- Dark luxury section divider slides

Design Rules:
- Avoid heavy effects and gradients
- Avoid dense information layouts
- Use serif typography as emotional focal point
- Keep imagery elegant and understated
- Use refined luxury magazine composition
- Maintain calm premium spacing
- Use muted sophisticated colors only
- Prioritize editorial atmosphere over corporate rigidity
